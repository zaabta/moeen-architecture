# MOEEN AI

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This document introduces MOEEN AI, an AI-native recruitment platform designed to modernize and automate the hiring process.

It defines the product vision, business context, project scope, and core objectives. This document serves as the starting point for all business, architectural, and technical documentation.

The purpose of this repository is to establish a single source of truth for everyone involved in the project, including product owners, software engineers, AI engineers, designers, QA engineers, DevOps engineers, and future contributors.

---

## 1.2 Product Overview

MOEEN AI is a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) recruitment platform that combines Artificial Intelligence with modern recruitment workflows.

Unlike traditional Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), MOEEN AI actively participates in the recruitment process through AI-powered resume analysis, candidate matching, intelligent recommendations, interview automation, and workflow management.

The platform helps organizations reduce hiring time while improving recruitment quality through data-driven decision making.

---

## 1.3 Vision

To become the leading AI-powered recruitment platform in the Middle East by enabling organizations to hire faster, smarter, and more objectively.

---

## 1.4 Mission

To empower recruiters and hiring managers with intelligent AI tools that automate repetitive recruitment tasks while keeping human decision-makers in full control of the hiring process.

---

## 1.5 Problem Statement

Recruitment remains one of the most expensive and time-consuming business processes.

Recruiters spend significant time reviewing resumes, screening candidates, scheduling interviews, communicating with applicants, and documenting evaluation results.

Traditional ATS platforms focus primarily on storing candidate information rather than assisting recruiters in making hiring decisions.

As hiring volumes increase, manual processes become difficult to scale, leading to slower hiring cycles, inconsistent evaluations, and poor candidate experiences.

---

## 1.6 Proposed Solution

MOEEN AI introduces an AI-assisted recruitment workflow.

The platform enables organizations to:

- Create and publish job openings.
- Receive candidate applications.
- Parse and analyze resumes automatically.
- Match candidates against job requirements.
- Generate AI-powered evaluation reports.
- Organize candidates using customizable recruitment pipelines.
- Support recruiters with intelligent recommendations.
- Automate repetitive recruitment tasks.

Future releases will extend the platform with AI interviews, workflow automation, and multi-agent collaboration.

---

## 1.7 Target Users

The platform is designed for multiple user groups.

### Organization

Companies using MOEEN AI to manage recruitment.

### Recruiter

Responsible for creating jobs, reviewing applications, and managing candidates.

### Hiring Manager

Reviews shortlisted candidates and makes final hiring decisions.

### Candidate

Applies for available job openings and participates in the hiring process.

### System Administrator

Manages platform configuration, tenants, billing, and global settings.
